MOVEMENT MODULE

MPU6050 / IMU

Measures:

  • acceleration
  • angular movement
  • movement patterns
  • arm motion

BALANCE MODULE

Pressure sensing platform

Measures:

  • weight distribution
  • left/right loading
  • pressure distribution
  • postural stability indicators

FORCE MODULE

Load cells

  • applied force
  • left/right force distribution
  • exercise performance

Handles:

  • sensor acquisition
  • processing
  • communication
  • system coordination

INTERFACE

Displays:

  • performance feedback

REHABO-SG / SYSTEM PROFILE

ABOUT REHABO-SG

A student-developed mobile robotic system designed to support post-stroke rehabilitation through guided exercises and non-invasive movement monitoring.

THE PROBLEM

Stroke can affect a person's ability to move, maintain balance, and perform coordinated upper-limb activities. Rehabilitation requires repeated exercises and continuous assessment of movement performance.

REHABO-SG / STUDY FRAMEWORK

RESEARCH

RESEARCH QUESTION

Can an integrated mobile robotic system provide consistent monitoring and feedback of upper-limb movement, and postural stability during rehabilitation exercises?

RESEARCH GAP

Existing rehabilitation technologies can provide exercise guidance or monitor individual aspects of movement. REHABO-SG explores the integration of guided upper-limb rehabilitation with simultaneous balance, and movement monitoring within a single mobile robotic platform.
